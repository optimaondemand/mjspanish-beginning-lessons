M/J Spanish, Beginning
Optima Academy Online · Semester 1 · ¡Bienvenidos!

¡Hola y bienvenidos! Welcome to your first year of Spanish. Over this semester you'll learn to greet people, introduce yourself and your family, count, talk about what you like, and describe who you are — all while discovering how much Spanish is already hiding in the world around you.

Everything is bite-size and interactive: short lessons, games, native audio, and gentle checks. Go at a steady pace, one lesson at a time. ¡Tú puedes!

HOW THIS COURSE WORKS
📅 18 modules, one per week
Each module has about 5 short lessons (~20 minutes each). Work through them in order — click "Mark as Done" at the top of each lesson so it counts as complete.
✅ A quick check to move on
Each module ends with a short Quick Check. You have unlimited attempts and keep your best score — reach 70% and the next module unlocks.
🎤 A few speaking projects
Three times this semester you'll record a short clip in Spanish (introduce yourself, your family, and a famous person). You can read from notes and re-record — it's about effort, not perfection.
📓 Habit check-ins
Every few modules, a quick check-in asks how your practice is going and what you can now say. Honest and short — that's all it takes.
HOW YOUR GRADE ADDS UP
Practice & Games 25% Quizzes 25% Habits & Check-ins 20% Projects & Oral 25% Semester Final 5%
