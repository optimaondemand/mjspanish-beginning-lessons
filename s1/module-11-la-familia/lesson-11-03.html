Lesson 11.03
The whole familia — escucha y repite
New vocabulary on a rule you already own — just hear it, say it, and sort it.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Here is your full family word set. The grammar is already yours — today is about the words and their sound.
Because you already know the -o/-a pattern, your only new job today is hearing each family word and saying it back. Then you'll sort them into el and la.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
say the core family words with clean Spanish vowels.
2
match each one to el or la by its ending and its meaning.

Press ▶ to hear each word, then say it out loud. Tap a card to flip it and see the meaning. Colors: blue = masculine (el), red = feminine (la).

🎮 PART 3 · SORT IT: EL or LA?

Drag each family word into the right bucket. Look at the last letter (and think about who it means). Correct drops lock in green; wrong ones bounce back so you can try again.
