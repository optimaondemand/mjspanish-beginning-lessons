## Items

### Q1 — The pattern, male → female (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** The word for brother is *hermano*. Using the pattern, what is the word for sister?
- **A) *hermana*** ✅
- B) *hermano*
- C) *hermane*
- D) *hermanos*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! Flip the -o to -a: *hermano* (brother) → **hermana** (sister)."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Remember the pattern — for a female relative, the word ends in **-a**: *hermana*."

---

### Q2 — Choose the article, -o (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Which article goes with *tío* (uncle)? It ends in **-o**.
- **A) *el tío*** ✅
- B) *la tío*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! -o → **el**, and an uncle is male: **el tío**."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*tío* ends in **-o**, so it's masculine: **el tío**."

---

### Q3 — Choose the article, -a (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Which article goes with *prima* (female cousin)? It ends in **-a**.
- A) *el prima*
- **B) *la prima*** ✅

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Muy bien! -a → **la**: **la prima**."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*prima* ends in **-a**, so it's feminine: **la prima**."

---

### Q4 — Meaning check (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You know *el abuelo* means grandfather. What does *la abuela* mean?
- **A) grandmother** ✅
- B) aunt
- C) daughter
- D) sister

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Exacto! *abuelo* (grandfather) → **abuela** (grandmother). Same pattern, every time."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "The -a form is the female one: *la abuela* = **grandmother**."

---

### Q5 — The special pair (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Most family words switch between -o and -a. Which pair is the special one that does **not** (both end in -e)?
- A) *hijo / hija*
- **B) *padre / madre*** ✅
- C) *tío / tía*
- D) *primo / prima*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! **padre / madre** end in -e, so the *article* tells you who: *el padre* (father), *la madre* (mother)."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Look for the pair ending in **-e**: **padre / madre**. The others all use the -o/-a switch."

---

### Q6 — "My" + family (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** How do you say **my sister**? (*hermana* = sister)
- A) *el hermana*
- **B) *mi hermana*** ✅
- C) *mi hermano*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Perfecto! *mi* means 'my' and works for anyone: **mi hermana**."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Use *mi* ('my') + the female word: **mi hermana**. (*mi hermano* would be my brother.)"

---

### Q7 — Pointing someone out (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You point to your father in a photo. Which sentence is right?
- **A) *Este es mi padre.*** ✅
- B) *Esta es mi madre.*
- C) *Este es mi hermana.*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *Este es…* introduces a male: **Este es mi padre.**"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For a male, use *Este es…* + the male word: **Este es mi padre.** (*Esta es mi madre* would introduce your mother.)"
