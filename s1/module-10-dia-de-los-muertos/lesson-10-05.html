📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · What it is

El Día de los Muertos blends Indigenous (Aztec/Nahua) and Catholic traditions. On Nov 1–2, families honor loved ones who have died — with joy, not fear.

2 · La ofrenda

The home display that welcomes loved ones back: la vela, la flor de cempasúchil, la calavera, el pan de muerto, el papel picado, la foto.

3 · In your world

It's celebrated across the US and shown in the movie Coco. UNESCO calls it part of the world's cultural heritage.
