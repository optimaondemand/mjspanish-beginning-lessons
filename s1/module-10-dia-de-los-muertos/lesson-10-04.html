LESSON 10.04 · SPANISH IN YOUR WORLD
You've probably already seen it
El Día de los Muertos isn't only in Mexico — it's in US cities, classrooms, and movies you know.
🌍 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
This holiday is a living part of US culture too — and you may already know it without realizing.

Millions of Mexican Americans keep the tradition alive across the United States. Cities from Los Angeles to San Antonio to Chicago hold community ofrendas, art shows, and parades in early November. And one of the most popular animated movies of the last decade is built entirely around it.
