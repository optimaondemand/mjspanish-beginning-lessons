## Items

### Q1 — Meaning / tone (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Which sentence best describes el Día de los Muertos?
- **A) A joyful time when families remember and honor loved ones who have died** ✅
- B) A scary night for costumes and candy
- C) A national sports holiday
- D) A day for exchanging birthday gifts

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! It's a warm, joyful remembering of loved ones — the heart of the whole week."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Remember the explainer: it's a **joyful** time to honor loved ones who have died — not about fear."

---

### Q2 — Origins (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** El Día de los Muertos grew from a blend of which two traditions?
- **A) Indigenous (Aztec/Nahua) practices and Catholic holy days** ✅
- B) Halloween and Thanksgiving
- C) Ancient Greek and Roman festivals
- D) French and German customs

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! Indigenous Mexican traditions blended with Catholic All Saints'/All Souls' Day."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "It blends **Indigenous (Aztec/Nahua)** practices with **Catholic** holy days — two traditions, one holiday."

---

### Q3 — When (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** When is el Día de los Muertos mainly celebrated?
- A) October 31
- **B) November 1 and 2** ✅
- C) December 25
- D) The first day of spring

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! November 1 and 2, right after Halloween — same season, different holiday."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "It's mainly **November 1 and 2**."

---

### Q4 — La ofrenda (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** What is *la ofrenda*?
- **A) A special home display that welcomes and honors loved ones who have died** ✅
- B) A kind of dance
- C) A city in Mexico
- D) A type of guitar

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Exacto! *La ofrenda* is the offering — the display with photos, food, flowers, and candles."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*La ofrenda* is the **home display** families build to welcome and honor their loved ones."

---

### Q5 — Vocabulary + gender (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Choose the correct article: **_____ vela** (the candle).
- **A) la vela** ✅
- B) el vela

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *vela* ends in -a → **la vela**."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*vela* ends in **-a**, so it's feminine: **la vela**."

---

### Q6 — Vocabulary + gender (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Choose the correct article: **_____ pan de muerto** ("bread of the dead").
- A) la pan de muerto
- **B) el pan de muerto** ✅

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! *pan* is masculine → **el pan de muerto**."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*pan* is masculine, so it's **el pan de muerto**."

---

### Q7 — Signature element (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Which is the signature orange flower of el Día de los Muertos, believed to help guide loved ones home?
- A) *la rosa* (the rose)
- **B) *la flor de cempasúchil* (the marigold)** ✅
- C) *el tulipán* (the tulip)
- D) *el girasol* (the sunflower)

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *La flor de cempasúchil* — the marigold. Its name comes from Nahuatl, an Indigenous language."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "It's *la flor de cempasúchil* — the bright orange **marigold**."
