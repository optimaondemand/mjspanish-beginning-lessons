Lesson 10.03
Los elementos de la ofrenda
Now that you know what an ofrenda means, here are the words for what goes on it.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
These are the new words — but the grammar is old news. You'll use the el / la gender rule from Module 2 to file each one.
Most of these follow the -o/-a pattern you already trust. A couple don't — and that's a good reminder that the rule has exceptions (just like el mapa and la mano from Module 2). Colors do the heavy lifting: blue = el, red = la.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
name, in Spanish, eight things that go on an ofrenda.
2
give the correct article (el or la) for each, and match each word to what it means.
