## Items

### Q1 — The pattern verb (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** To say where a place is *located*, Module 9 used which verb?
- A) *es*
- **B) *está*** ✅
- C) *soy*
- D) *tiene*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *está* = "it is (located)." That's the anchor of the whole map pattern."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For *location*, Spanish uses ***está***: *Monterrey está al norte…*"

---

### Q2 — The hinge word (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** In "Guadalajara está al oeste **___** la Ciudad de México," which little word means "of"?
- A) *al*
- **B) *de*** ✅
- C) *en*
- D) *y*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! *de* = "of / from." *Al oeste **de**…* = "to the west **of**…""
*Feedback (incorrect):* "The word that means "of" is ***de***: *al oeste **de** México*."

---

### Q3 — Build the sentence (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Monterrey is **north** of Mexico City. Choose the correct completion: "Monterrey está ___ de la Ciudad de México."
- **A) al norte** ✅
- B) al sur
- C) al este
- D) al oeste

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Muy bien! North = *al norte*."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "North is ***al norte***. (*sur* = south, *este* = east, *oeste* = west.)"

---

### Q4 — State → capital (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** What is the capital of **Jalisco**?
- A) Monterrey
- **B) Guadalajara** ✅
- C) Mérida
- D) Hermosillo

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! The capital of Jalisco is **Guadalajara**."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Jalisco's capital is **Guadalajara**. (Monterrey → Nuevo León; Mérida → Yucatán; Hermosillo → Sonora.)"

---

### Q5 — The national capital (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** What is the capital city of the whole country of Mexico?
- **A) la Ciudad de México** ✅
- B) Guadalajara
- C) Cancún
- D) Monterrey

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! **La Ciudad de México** (Mexico City) is the national capital — like Washington, D.C."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Mexico's national capital is **la Ciudad de México** (Mexico City)."

---

### Q6 — Gender of geography words (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Which of these geography words is **masculine** (takes *el*)?
- A) *la capital*
- B) *la ciudad*
- **C) *el mapa*** ✅
- D) *la frontera*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! ***el mapa*** is masculine — a friendly exception (it ends in -a but takes *el*)."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "***el mapa*** is masculine. The other three (*capital, ciudad, frontera*) are feminine → *la*."

---

### Q7 — Spanish on the US map / culture link (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** The US state name **Montana** comes straight from the Spanish word *montaña*. What does it mean?
- A) meadow
- **B) mountain** ✅
- C) border
- D) river

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Exacto! *montaña* = "mountain" — perfect for a state full of the Rocky Mountains."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*montaña* means **mountain**. (Río Grande = "big river"; frontera = "border.")"
