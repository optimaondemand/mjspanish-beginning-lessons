📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · The pattern

X está al [norte / sur / este / oeste] de Y — "X is to the ___ of Y." The little word de means "of."

2 · Estados y capitales

Mexico is a country of states, each with a capital: Jalisco→Guadalajara, Nuevo León→Monterrey, Yucatán→Mérida… and the national capital, la Ciudad de México.

3 · Spanish on the US map

Montana ("mountain"), Río Grande ("big river"), El Paso ("the pass"), Santa Fe ("holy faith") — US places with Spanish names.

🎮 REVIEW GAME · STATE & CAPITAL MEMORY
