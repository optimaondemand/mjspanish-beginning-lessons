Lesson 9.03
Estados y capitales de México
New words on a pattern you already own — listen, repeat, and match.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Time to meet the actual places. These states and capitals are the new vocabulary — and you'll drop them straight into yesterday's está al… de pattern.
These are place names, so most don't follow the -o/-a gender rule — a city or state is just its name. But the everyday geography words that go with them (el mapa, la capital…) do have gender, and they're color-coded below as always.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
say the names of several Mexican states and their capitals with clean Spanish vowels.
2
match each state to its capital city.
3
use a few color-coded geography words: el mapa, el estado, el país, la capital, la ciudad, la frontera.
