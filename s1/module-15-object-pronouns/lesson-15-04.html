🌍 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
English borrowed hundreds of everyday words straight from Spanish — and you already know what most of them mean.

All year, the "Spanish in Your World" cards have shown you the Spanish hiding inside English: in your food, your backyard, your maps, and the old West. Today is a friendly checkpoint. Play the game, then remember: a few of these loanwords show up on your Module 15 Quick Check for a grade. Read them out loud — they are all Spanish:

rodeoplazapatiofiestamosquitosiestacanyonranchlassochocolate

Every one of them started as a Spanish word. Some even traveled a longer road — through Spanish from the languages of the Americas — before English picked them up. Let us find out what they really mean.
