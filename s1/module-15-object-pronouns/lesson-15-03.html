Lesson 15.03
Hear the "to-whom" word in real sentences
New grammar on words you already know — nothing new to memorize.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Let us take yesterday's five words and hear them inside full sentences.
Every sentence below uses only words you already own — la maestra, el libro, el taco, el chocolate, el regalo, los tacos. Your only new job is hearing the little to-whom word and noticing where it sits: right before the verb da (gives).
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
say a full sentence with the "to-whom" word in the right place.
2
match each little word to the person who receives the action.
🔊 PART 1 · ESCUCHA Y REPITE (LISTEN & REPEAT)

Press ▶ to hear each sentence, then say it out loud. Tap a card to flip it and see the meaning. Every sentence uses the verb da (gives) and changes only the to-whom word.

La maestra me da el libro.
tap for meaning
The teacher gives the book to me.
La maestra te da el taco.
tap for meaning
The teacher gives the taco to you.
La maestra le da el regalo.
tap for meaning
The teacher gives the gift to him / to her.
La maestra nos da el chocolate.
tap for meaning
The teacher gives the chocolate to us.
La maestra les da los tacos.
tap for meaning
The teacher gives the tacos to them.
💡 Notice: the verb (da) and the thing given change nothing about the pattern. Only the to-whom word swaps — and it always sits right before da.
🎮 PART 2 · MATCH: WORD → WHO RECEIVES IT

Drag each little word to the person it points to. Correct matches lock in green; wrong drops bounce back.
