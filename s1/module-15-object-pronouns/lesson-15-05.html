📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · The "to-whom" word

Every sentence can have a person who receives the action. Spanish packs that into one small word: me, te, le, nos, les.

2 · Before the verb

The "to-whom" word goes right before the verb: La maestra me da el libro = the teacher gives the book to me.

3 · Spanish is in English

Loanwords like rodeo, plaza, patio, mosquito, chocolate are Spanish words English kept — free vocabulary you already own.
