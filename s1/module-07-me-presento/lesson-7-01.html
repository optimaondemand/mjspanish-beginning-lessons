Lesson 7.01
This week you introduce yourself — for real
You already own all the pieces. This week you snap them together.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You can already say hello, tell someone your name, and say goodbye. What if you put them in a row and added one warm phrase in the middle?
That's a whole introduction — the kind you'd give on a first day of school in Guadalajara or at a family party in Miami. This week has almost no brand-new grammar. Instead you learn to chain the chunks you already know into one smooth self-introduction, and add a couple of friendly phrases like mucho gusto along the way.

📚 PART 1 · WARM UP — THE PIECES YOU ALREADY HAVE

Here are four things you can already say in Spanish. Read each one out loud with your clean Module 1 vowels:

¡Hola! — Hello
Me llamo… — My name is…
Soy de… — I'm from…
Hasta luego — See you later
💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · NO WRONG ANSWER
You walk into a new classroom in Mexico. Which of these would you probably say first?
🔍 PART 2 · WATCH THE PIECES LINE UP

Now watch what happens when we put those same chunks in a sensible order. Read this little introduction out loud — you already understand every word:

¡Hola!
Me llamo Ana.
Soy de México.
Mucho gusto.
Hasta luego.

That's a complete introduction — greeting, name, where I'm from, a warm "nice to meet you," and a goodbye. Only one line is new to you: Mucho gusto ("nice to meet you"). Everything else you already had in your pocket.

💡 The big idea of the week: learning a language isn't only about new words — it's about combining the words you already have. This week you turn five little pieces into one confident introduction.
