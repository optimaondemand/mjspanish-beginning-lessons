## Items

### Q1 — Recipe order (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** In a Spanish self-introduction, which step comes **first**?
- A) Say goodbye (*Hasta luego*)
- **B) Greet the person (*¡Hola!*)** ✅
- C) Say "nice to meet you" (*Mucho gusto*)
- D) Ask a question

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! You open with a greeting — step 1 of the recipe."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Remember the recipe: it starts with a greeting like *¡Hola!*"

---

### Q2 — Recipe order (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Put these in the right order: (1) *Soy de Texas* · (2) *¡Hola!* · (3) *Me llamo Sara*.
- **A) ¡Hola! → Me llamo Sara → Soy de Texas** ✅
- B) Soy de Texas → ¡Hola! → Me llamo Sara
- C) Me llamo Sara → Soy de Texas → ¡Hola!

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Perfecto! Greet, then name, then where you're from."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "The recipe goes greeting → name → where you're from: *¡Hola! / Me llamo Sara / Soy de Texas.*"

---

### Q3 — New phrase meaning (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** What does **Mucho gusto** mean?
- A) Goodbye
- **B) Nice to meet you** ✅
- C) Where are you from?
- D) Good morning

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! *Mucho gusto* = 'nice to meet you.'"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*Mucho gusto* is how you say 'nice to meet you' — step 4 of the recipe."

---

### Q4 — Natural gender: encantado / encantada (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** A **boy** is meeting someone new and wants to say "delighted." He says:
- **A) Encantado** ✅
- B) Encantada

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! A boy says *encantado* (ends in -o). A girl says *encantada*."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "You match the ending to yourself: a boy says *encantado* (-o), a girl says *encantada* (-a)."

---

### Q5 — Natural gender: encantado / encantada (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Sofía (a girl) wants to say "delighted." She says:
- A) Encantado
- **B) Encantada** ✅

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Muy bien! Sofía is female, so she says *encantada* (ends in -a) — the -o/-a pattern with people again."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Sofía matches the ending to herself: a girl says *encantada* (-a)."

---

### Q6 — Reply phrase (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Someone says **Mucho gusto** to you. A natural way to reply is:
- **A) Igualmente (likewise)** ✅
- B) Buenos días
- C) Me llamo

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Exacto! *Igualmente* ('likewise') answers *mucho gusto* perfectly."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*Igualmente* means 'likewise' — the natural reply to *mucho gusto*."

---

### Q7 — Asking where someone is from / register (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You're meeting your friend's grandmother and want to ask, politely, where she's from. You say:
- A) ¿De dónde eres? (friendly *tú*)
- **B) ¿De dónde es usted? (polite *usted*)** ✅
- C) Soy de México.

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! With an adult you respect, use the polite *usted*: *¿De dónde es usted?*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "With an adult, use the polite *usted* form: *¿De dónde es usted?* (*Soy de…* answers the question — it doesn't ask it.)"
