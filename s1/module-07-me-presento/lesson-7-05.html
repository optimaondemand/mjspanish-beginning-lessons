📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · The recipe

Greet → name → where you're from → nice to meet you → goodbye. Five steps, same order, every time.

2 · The warm phrases

Mucho gusto works for everyone. A boy says encantado; a girl says encantada. Reply with igualmente.

3 · Ask back

¿De dónde eres? (friendly) or ¿De dónde es usted? (polite) turns your intro into a real conversation.

🎮 REVIEW GAME · MATCH THE PHRASE TO ITS MEANING
✅ SELF-CHECK · ARE YOU READY?

These mirror the Quick Check. If you can do these, you're ready. Practice as many times as you like.

🎤 Your task: record your own introduction

Head to the Module 7 — Self-Introduction assignment in Canvas and use the media recorder to record a short clip (about 45–60 seconds). This is graded on effort and completeness, not perfection — and you may read from your notes.

  1. Greet: ¡Hola! or Buenos días.
  2. Name: Me llamo…
  3. Where you're from: Soy de…
  4. Nice to meet you: Mucho gusto (or encantado/encantada).
  5. Goodbye: Hasta luego.

💡 Write your five lines on paper first, practice out loud twice, then record. Speak slowly and give every vowel its clean Spanish sound.

📝 And: the Module 7 Quick Check

Head to the Module 7 Quick Check in Canvas — a short, friendly quiz on the introduction recipe and phrases.

  • Just a few questions — the same ideas you just practiced.
  • Score about 70% to unlock the next module.
  • Unlimited tries — retake it as often as you want, and your best score counts.
✓

Before you go: click "Mark as Done", record your introduction, and open the Module 7 Quick Check. ¡Buena suerte! (Good luck!)

💪 ONE IDEA TO CARRY OUT

You can now introduce yourself to a real person in Spanish — greeting, name, roots, and a warm goodbye. That's a genuine, useful skill, and it's yours.
