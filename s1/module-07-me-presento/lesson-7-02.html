Lesson 7.02 · The heart of the module
A five-step recipe for introducing yourself
Same five steps every time. Learn the shape, and you can introduce yourself to anyone.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
An introduction isn't a random pile of phrases — it follows a friendly, predictable order.
Think of it like a recipe. Five simple steps, in the same order, and you get a warm, complete introduction every time. Four of the five steps use words you already know from earlier modules — only the "nice to meet you" step is new (and you'll practice it fully tomorrow).
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
name the five steps of a Spanish self-introduction, in order.
2
fill each step with a phrase you already know.
3
put a jumbled introduction back into a sensible order.
📚 PART 1 · THE FIVE STEPS

Here is the recipe. Read each step out loud. Notice how most of it is already yours — the gray tag means "you learned this earlier," and the gold tag means "new this week."

1
¡Hola! / Buenos días.
Greet the person. (Hello / Good morning.)
from Modules 1 & 6
2
Me llamo… / Soy…
Say your name. (My name is… / I am…)
from Module 1
3
Soy de…
Say where you're from. (I'm from…)
from Module 1
4
Mucho gusto.
Say it was nice to meet them. (Nice to meet you.)
new this week
5
Hasta luego. / Adiós.
Say goodbye. (See you later. / Goodbye.)
from Module 6
💡 Only step 4 is brand new. Four of the five steps are pieces you already owned — that's what "new grammar on words you know" feels like: comfortable, not scary.
✍️ PART 2 · FILL IN THE RECIPE — A WORKED EXAMPLE

Watch how a student named Miguel from Puerto Rico fills the five steps with his own information:

1. ¡Hola!
2. Me llamo Miguel.
3. Soy de Puerto Rico.
4. Mucho gusto.
5. Hasta luego.

Now picture your own version. You'd keep steps 1, 4, and 5 exactly the same — and just swap in your name and your place in steps 2 and 3. That's the beauty of a recipe: the shape stays, the filling is yours.

✅ PART 3 · GUIDED PRACTICE

Use the recipe. Tap around and learn from the feedback — this is just practice.

Which step is which?
You want to start your introduction. Which phrase belongs first?
Which phrase tells someone where you're from?
Greeting → name → where you're from → ? → goodbye. What fills the blank (step 4)?
🧠 PART 4 · CHECK YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Make sure the recipe stuck. Practice as many times as you like.

✓

Before you go: click "Mark as Done" at the top of this page. Next, Lesson 7.03 gives you the new phrases — with sound — to fill step 4 with confidence.

💪 ONE IDEA TO CARRY OUT

Say the recipe to yourself: greet → name → where I'm from → nice to meet you → goodbye. Five steps, same order, every time.
