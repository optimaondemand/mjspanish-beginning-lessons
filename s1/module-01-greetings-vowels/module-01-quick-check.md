## Items

### Q1 — Multiple choice (vowels)
**How many vowels does Spanish have, and how do they behave?**
- A. Five vowels, and each one makes the same sound every time. ✅
- B. Five vowels, but each changes sound depending on the word.
- C. The same shifting vowels as English.
- D. It depends on the region.

*Feedback (correct):* Exactly — five fixed sounds. That's the secret to reading Spanish out loud.
*Feedback (incorrect):* Revisit Lesson 1.02 — Spanish has five vowels, and each keeps one fixed sound.

---

### Q2 — Multiple choice (vowels)
**The Spanish letter A (as in *casa*) always sounds like the vowel in which English word?**
- A. cake
- B. father ✅
- C. about
- D. cat

*Feedback (correct):* Sí — the "ah" in *father*, every time.
*Feedback (incorrect):* Spanish A is the "ah" in *father* — not the "ay" in cake.

---

### Q3 — Multiple choice (vowels)
**Which statement about Spanish vowels is TRUE?**
- A. The final vowel is usually silent, like English silent e.
- B. You pronounce every vowel — Spanish has no silent vowels. ✅
- C. Only stressed vowels are pronounced.
- D. Vowels are optional in fast speech.

*Feedback (correct):* Right — say every vowel. *Estudiante* = e-s-t-u-d-i-a-n-t-e, all five vowels sounded.
*Feedback (incorrect):* In Spanish you pronounce every vowel — there are no silent vowels.

---

### Q4 — Fill in the blank (greetings)
**Complete the greeting. To say "hello" to anyone, any time of day, you say ¡______!**
- Accepted answers (case-insensitive): `Hola`, `hola`, `¡Hola!`, `hola!`

*Feedback (correct):* ¡Hola! — your first Spanish word.
*Feedback (incorrect):* The all-purpose Spanish "hello" is **¡Hola!**

---

### Q5 — Multiple choice (greetings)
**You want to ask a classmate their name. What do you say?**
- A. Me llamo Ana.
- B. ¿Cómo te llamas? ✅
- C. Mucho gusto.
- D. Nos vemos.

*Feedback (correct):* Correct — *¿Cómo te llamas?* = "What's your name?"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "What's your name?" is **¿Cómo te llamas?** (*Me llamo…* is how you answer it.)

---

### Q6 — Multiple choice (greetings — register)
**You greet your teacher and want to ask, politely, how they are. Which is the respectful form?**
- A. ¿Cómo estás?
- B. ¿Cómo te llamas?
- C. ¿Cómo está usted? ✅
- D. Hasta luego.

*Feedback (correct):* Yes — with a teacher or an adult, use **usted**: *¿Cómo está usted?*
*Feedback (incorrect):* For a teacher or adult, the respectful form uses *usted*: **¿Cómo está usted?**

---

### Q7 — Multiple choice (farewells)
**Which phrase would you use to say goodbye?**
- A. Buenos días
- B. Hasta luego ✅
- C. Mucho gusto
- D. ¿Cómo estás?

*Feedback (correct):* Right — *Hasta luego* = "see you later." (*Adiós* and *Nos vemos* work too.)
*Feedback (incorrect):* A goodbye is **Hasta luego** ("see you later"). The others are greetings or a first-meeting phrase.
