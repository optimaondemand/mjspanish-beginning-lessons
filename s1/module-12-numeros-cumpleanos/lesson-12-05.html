📝 THE WHOLE WEEK ON ONE CARD
1–15. Their own words: uno, dos, tres… catorce, quince. No shortcut — you just learn them.
16–29. Glued into one word: dieciséis, veintidós. The pieces are numbers you already know.
31–99. Ten + y + ones: cuarenta y cinco (45), noventa y nueve (99).
Your age. One chunk: Tengo… años — you have your years. Ask with ¿Cuántos años tienes?
The party. el cumpleaños, la fiesta, el pastel, el regalo, la vela, la piñata, soplar las velas.
The culture. piñata (borrowed whole into English), quinceañera (the 15th!), and Feliz cumpleaños a ti.
🎮 PLAY 1 · MATCH THE NUMBER TO ITS DIGITS

Drag each Spanish number onto the matching numeral. Build it in your head first — ten, then y, then ones.

🎮 PLAY 2 · BIRTHDAY MEMORY MATCH

Flip two cards to pair each Spanish party word with the right picture. Matches stay face-up.
