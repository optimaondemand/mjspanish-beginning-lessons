¡Es una fiesta! — birthday words
New vocabulary on grammar you already own — hear it, say it, and use your el/la rule.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Here are the words for a birthday party. The grammar is already yours — today is about the words and their sound.
You already know the -o → el and -a → la pattern from Module 2. So as each party word appears, notice its ending and its color — the gender rule is doing the work for you again.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
say the core birthday words with clean Spanish vowels.
2
match each thing to el or la and describe a party.

⭐ Notice el pastel. It ends in -l, not -o — so its ending doesn't announce its gender the way -o/-a words do. Just learn it with its article: el pastel. (You'll meet more words like this all year.)
🎂 PART 2 · PUT IT IN A SENTENCE

You already have hay ("there is / there are") from Lucía's family last week, and now you have numbers. Watch how naturally the party comes together:

Hoy es el cumpleaños de mi hermana. Es una fiesta grande. Hay un pastel con doce velas y muchos regalos. Mi hermana va a soplar las velas. ¡Feliz cumpleaños!

(Today is my sister's birthday. It's a big party. There is a cake with twelve candles and lots of gifts. My sister is going to blow out the candles. Happy birthday!)
