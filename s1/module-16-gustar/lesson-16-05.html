📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · A backwards verb

gustar means "to be pleasing." So Spanish says the thing is pleasing to you: Me gusta la pizza = "I like pizza."

2 · gusta or gustan

Look at the thing. One thing (or an action) → gusta. More than one → gustan. Me gusta la música; me gustan los tacos.

3 · It is already yours

The things you like — tacos, salsa, chocolate, Spanish music — are already part of your world. Now you can name them.
