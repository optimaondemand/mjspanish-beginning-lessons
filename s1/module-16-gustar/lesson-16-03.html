New words on grammar you now own. Color-coded, with audio.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You have the gustar pattern — now here are the fun things to plug into it.
Meet a small set of foods, music, and activities. Some you have already seen this year. Remember the colors: blue = masculine (el), red = feminine (la). Activities are verbs, so they get their own green tag.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
say a set of "things to like" with clean Spanish vowels.
2
plug each one into me gusta or me gustan.

🔊 PART 2 · HEAR THE WHOLE SENTENCE

Now put a word inside the pattern. Notice how the ending changes for one thing vs. more than one:

Me gusta la música.
I like music (one thing → gusta)
Me gustan los tacos.
I like tacos (more than one → gustan)
