## Items

### Q1 — Concept (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Why is *gustar* called a "backwards" verb?
- **A) It means "to be pleasing," so the *thing* you like is the star of the sentence** ✅
- B) It is spelled backwards
- C) You only use it at the end of a sentence
- D) It has no subject

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *Me gusta la pizza* really means 'pizza is pleasing to me' — the thing is the star."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*gustar* = 'to be pleasing.' The thing you like is the subject, so the sentence flips around."

---

### Q2 — gusta with one thing (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Choose the correct word: **Me _____ la música.** (I like music)
- **A) gusta** ✅
- B) gustan

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! *la música* is one thing → **gusta**."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*la música* is singular (one thing), so use **gusta**: *Me gusta la música.*"

---

### Q3 — gustan with plural (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Choose the correct word: **Me _____ los tacos.** (I like tacos)
- A) gusta
- **B) gustan** ✅

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Muy bien! *los tacos* is more than one → **gustan**."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*los tacos* is plural (more than one), so use **gustan**: *Me gustan los tacos.*"

---

### Q4 — gusta with an action (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You want to say "I like **to dance**." Which is correct?
- **A) Me gusta bailar** ✅
- B) Me gustan bailar

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Exacto! Liking to *do* something always uses **gusta**: *Me gusta bailar.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Liking an action always takes **gusta**, even one action: *Me gusta bailar.*"

---

### Q5 — meaning of the "to-whom" word (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** In **Me gustan los tacos**, what does the little word *me* mean?
- **A) to me** ✅
- B) to them
- C) to us
- D) to you

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *me* = to me — from Module 15. It shows who the tacos are pleasing to."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*me* = to me (remember Module 15). *Me gustan los tacos* = the tacos are pleasing to me."

---

### Q6 — build the sentence (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Which sentence correctly says "I like chocolate"? (*el chocolate* is one thing)
- **A) Me gusta el chocolate** ✅
- B) Me gustan el chocolate
- C) Gusta me el chocolate
- D) El chocolate me gustan

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Perfecto! One thing → *gusta*, and *me* comes first: **Me gusta el chocolate.**"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*el chocolate* is one thing → *gusta*, and the *me* goes first: **Me gusta el chocolate.**"

---

### Q7 — light culture reinforcement (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** When you say *me gusta la salsa*, you might mean a food or a music. What does the Spanish word *salsa* mean?
- **A) sauce (and it is also a style of music)** ✅
- B) spicy
- C) a dance floor
- D) red

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *salsa* means 'sauce' — and the lively music took the same name."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*salsa* is Spanish for 'sauce'; the dance music borrowed the name."
