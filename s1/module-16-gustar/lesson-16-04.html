LESSON 16.04 · SPANISH IN YOUR WORLD
The Food & Music You Already Love
Say what you like — and notice how much of it is already Spanish.
🌍 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
A lot of the food and music kids across the US already love comes straight from the Spanish-speaking world.

Now that you can say me gusta and me gustan, look at what you are naming. Tacos, salsa, chocolate — these are not just English words. They are Spanish words English kept, for foods that came from Mexico and the wider Spanish-speaking world. Read them out loud — you already like them, and now you can name them in Spanish:

el tacola salsael chocolatela tortillael tomateel aguacate

And the music — salsa is both a sauce and a style of music, and Spanish-language songs fill the US charts. When you say me gusta la música, you are talking about a world you already live in.

✅ APPLY IT · SAY WHAT YOU LIKE
Use gusta (one thing) or gustan (more than one) with these foods you now know.
You love tacos. How do you say "I like tacos"?
You want to say "I like salsa (the sauce)." Which is right?
Which of these words is Spanish for a "sauce" AND the name of a dance music?
★ WHY THIS MATTERS

The things you like — the food on your plate, the songs in your ears — are already full of Spanish. Learning to say me gusta is not learning about a distant place. It is learning to name what you already love, in the language it came from.
