## Items

### Q1 — ESTAR form: yo (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Complete the sentence: "Yo _____ bien." (*I am well.*)
- **A) estoy** ✅
- B) estás
- C) está
- D) están

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *Yo estoy* = 'I am.' *Yo estoy bien.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For *yo* ('I'), the ESTAR form is **estoy**: *Yo estoy bien.*"

---

### Q2 — ESTAR form: tú (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You already say this greeting! Complete it: "¿Cómo _____ tú?" (*How are you?*)
- **A) estás** ✅
- B) estoy
- C) estamos
- D) es

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! *¿Cómo estás?* — you've used ESTAR since Module 6 without knowing its name."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For *tú* ('you'), use **estás**: *¿Cómo estás?*"

---

### Q3 — ESTAR form: nosotros (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Complete the sentence: "Nosotros _____ aquí." (*We are here.*)
- A) estoy
- **B) estamos** ✅
- C) están
- D) está

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Muy bien! *Nosotros estamos* = 'we are.' *Nosotros estamos aquí.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For *nosotros* ('we'), the form is **estamos**: *Nosotros estamos aquí.*"

---

### Q4 — SER vs. ESTAR: identity/role (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You want to say **"She is a doctor"** (her job — a lasting fact). Which is right?
- **A) Ella es doctora** ✅
- B) Ella está doctora
- C) Ella estoy doctora
- D) Ella están doctora

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Exacto! A job/role is who she IS, so it's SER: *Ella es doctora.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "A role is a lasting fact → SER. *Ella es doctora.* (ESTAR is for feelings and location.)"

---

### Q5 — SER vs. ESTAR: location (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You want to say **"Miami is in Florida"** (where it is). Which is right?
- A) Miami es en Florida
- **B) Miami está en Florida** ✅
- C) Miami soy en Florida
- D) Miami son en Florida

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *Where* something is = ESTAR: *Miami está en Florida.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Location is always ESTAR: *Miami está en Florida.* (SER would be wrong here.)"

---

### Q6 — SER vs. ESTAR: feeling (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You want to say **"I am tired right now"** (a feeling this moment). Which is right?
- A) Yo soy cansado / cansada
- **B) Yo estoy cansado / cansada** ✅
- C) Yo es cansado / cansada
- D) Yo somos cansado / cansada

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Perfecto! A feeling right now = ESTAR: *Yo estoy cansado / cansada.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "A feeling that can change is ESTAR, not SER: *Yo estoy cansado / cansada.*"

---

### Q7 — The deciding idea (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** What's the quickest way to choose between SER and ESTAR?
- A) SER for people, ESTAR for objects
- **B) SER for who/what something IS (lasting facts); ESTAR for how it feels or WHERE it is** ✅
- C) They mean the same thing, so it doesn't matter
- D) ESTAR is only used in questions

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! Lasting fact (name, origin, role) → SER. Feeling or place → ESTAR. That one question decides it."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Ask: a lasting fact (SER) or a right-now feeling/location (ESTAR)? That's the line."
