LESSON 14.05
Bring the week together
Two verbs for "to be," and one clear line between them. You've got this.
📝 QUICK RECAP · THE TWO VERBS SIDE BY SIDE

SER (from Module 13)

soy · eres · es · somos · son
WHO / WHAT you are: name, origin, role. Yo soy estudiante. Soy de México.

ESTAR (this week)

estoy · estás · está · estamos · están
HOW you feel / WHERE things are. Estoy bien. Miami está en Florida.

The deciding question, every time: a lasting fact (SER), or a right-now feeling or a place (ESTAR)?
