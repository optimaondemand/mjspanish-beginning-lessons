Lesson 8.02
Los colores — escucha y repite
New words, on grammar you already own. Just listen, look, and say.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Today is a pure vocabulary day — no new grammar at all. Just meet the colors, hear them, and say them.
Because there's nothing new to figure out grammatically, you can relax and focus on sound. Press ▶ on each color, look at its swatch, and say the word out loud with clean Spanish vowels. Tap a card to check the meaning.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
name ten common colors in Spanish.
2
pronounce each one clearly, giving every vowel its steady Spanish sound.

💪 ONE IDEA TO CARRY OUT
