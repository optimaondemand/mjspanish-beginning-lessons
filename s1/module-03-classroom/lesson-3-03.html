LESSON 3.03
Name the people and things around you
New words — but the el/la rule you already own does the sorting.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Today is pure vocabulary — no new grammar. Every word rides on the gender rule from Module 2, so you already know how to handle it.
You'll meet the people in a classroom first — where -o/-a genuinely tells you male or female — then the objects, where gender is just the group a word belongs to. Listen to each, say it back, and tap to check the meaning.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
name the main people and objects of a classroom in Spanish, with clean vowels.
2
give each one the right article, el or la, by reading its ending.
👥 PART 1 · THE PEOPLE — WHERE GENDER MEANS MALE OR FEMALE

Start with people, because here the -o/-a ending really does match: a male teacher is el profesor, a female teacher is la profesora. Press ▶, say it, then tap to flip.

💡 Notice: estudiante doesn't change its ending — only the article moves: el estudiante (a male student) / la estudiante (a female student). A few people-words work that way.
📚 PART 2 · THE OBJECTS — READ THE ENDING

For objects, gender is just the group a word belongs to — la mesa isn't "a girl table." Use the ending: -o → el, -a → la.
