📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Some of the most ordinary English words — including one you use at school every day — walked straight in from Spanish.
You'll meet a few of these loanwords, play the "Spanish hiding in English" game, and then put this week's commands and objects together the way a real class does.
🌎 Spanish in Your World

Cafeteria, ranch, corral — all Spanish

When English-speaking settlers moved across the American Southwest, they met a world already run in Spanish — on ranches, in courtyards, at coffee counters — and they borrowed the words wholesale. Here are three you already own:

cafetería → cafeteria
rancho → ranch
corral → corral

Your school cafeteria is a Spanish word: cafetería means a place that serves coffee and food. Say it with your clean Spanish vowels — kah-feh-teh-REE-ah — and you can hear the Spanish hiding inside the everyday English.
