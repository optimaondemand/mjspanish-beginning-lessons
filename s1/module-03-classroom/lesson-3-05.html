📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · Commands you follow

A command tells you to do something: Escuche (listen), Mire (look), Abra el libro (open the book). Your job is to understand and respond.

2 · People & objects

You can name the room: el libro, la silla, la puerta, el profesor / la profesora — each with the article its ending predicts.

3 · Spanish in your world

Everyday English words are Spanish: cafetería → cafeteria, rancho → ranch, corral → corral.
