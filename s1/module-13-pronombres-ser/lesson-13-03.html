LESSON 13.03
Yo soy… — words that finish the sentence
New words on the verb you just learned: soy + who you are.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned soy = "I am." Today you get the words to finish the thought — and you already understand most of them.

These are words for jobs and roles: doctor, profesor, estudiante, artista, actor. Say them out loud and something jumps out — they look almost exactly like English. That's not a coincidence, and tomorrow you'll find out why. For today, just meet them and notice their gender.

🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
say five job/role words with clean Spanish vowels.
2
choose the male or female form (-o / -a) for a person.
3
combine them with SER: yo soy estudiante, ella es doctora.

Press ▶ to hear each word, then say it out loud. Tap a card to flip it and see the meaning. Remember the colors from Module 2: blue = masculine, red = feminine. For people, the ending usually matches the person — a male doctor, a female doctora.

💬 PART 2 · SNAP THEM ONTO SER

Now the payoff. Take a SER form from yesterday and add a role word. No memorizing a new sentence — you're just clicking two known pieces together:

  • Yo soy estudiante. — I am a student.
  • Tú eres artista. — You are an artist.
  • Ella es doctora. — She is a doctor.
  • Él es profesor. — He is a teacher.
💡 Notice: Spanish doesn't use "a" here. It's just soy estudiante — literally "I am student." One less word to worry about.
