## Items

### Q1 — Concept of conjugation (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Module 13 said English does this too, in "I **am**, you **are**, she **is**." To *conjugate* a verb means to:
- **A) change its shape so it matches who is doing it** ✅
- B) translate it into English
- C) always say it with the word *yo*
- D) spell it with an accent mark

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! Conjugating = changing the verb's shape for the person, exactly like *am / are / is* in English."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Think of *am / are / is* — same verb, different shapes for different people. That's conjugating."

---

### Q2 — Subject pronoun: the polite "you" (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You're speaking to an adult you want to show respect to. Which word for "you" is the safe, polite default in most of Latin America?
- A) *tú*
- **B) *usted*** ✅
- C) *yo*
- D) *nosotros*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Correcto! *usted* is the respectful 'you' — the safe choice with adults and strangers."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "Use *tú* with a friend, but *usted* to be polite with an adult. When in doubt, *usted*."

---

### Q3 — SER form: yo (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Complete the sentence: "Yo _____ estudiante." (*I am a student.*)
- **A) soy** ✅
- B) eres
- C) es
- D) son

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Perfecto! *Yo soy* = 'I am.' *Yo soy estudiante.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For *yo* ('I'), the SER form is **soy**: *Yo soy estudiante.*"

---

### Q4 — SER form: él / ella / usted (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Complete the sentence: "Ella _____ de México." (*She is from Mexico.*)
- A) soy
- B) somos
- **C) es** ✅
- D) eres

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Muy bien! One form — **es** — covers *él*, *ella*, and *usted*. *Ella es de México.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For *ella* ('she'), use **es**. That one form also covers *él* and *usted*."

---

### Q5 — SER form: nosotros (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You and a friend, together. Complete: "Nosotros _____ de Florida." (*We are from Florida.*)
- A) soy
- **B) somos** ✅
- C) son
- D) es

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *Nosotros somos* = 'we are.' *Nosotros somos de Florida.*"
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For *nosotros* ('we'), the form is **somos**: *Nosotros somos de Florida.*"

---

### Q6 — SER for identity (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** Which sentence correctly says **"He is a teacher"**?
- A) *Él soy profesor*
- **B) *Él es profesor*** ✅
- C) *Él somos profesor*
- D) *Él eres profesor*

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Exacto! *Él es profesor.* Use **es** for 'he,' and no word for 'a' is needed."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "For 'he is,' use **es**: *Él es profesor.* (Spanish skips the word 'a' here.)"

---

### Q7 — Identity cognate / culture link (Multiple Choice)
**Prompt:** You see *la doctora* on a Spanish sign and understand it instantly, because it looks and means almost the same as English *doctor*. Module 13 called a word like this a:
- **A) cognate (a near-twin of an English word)** ✅
- B) subject pronoun
- C) conjugation
- D) farewell expression

*Feedback (correct):* "¡Sí! *doctor / doctora* is a **cognate** of English *doctor* — free comprehension. Identity words are full of them."
*Feedback (incorrect):* "*doctora* is a **cognate** — the near-twin of English *doctor*, so you understand it for free."
