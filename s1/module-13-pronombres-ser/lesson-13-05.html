LESSON 13.05
Bring the week together
One idea (conjugation), one set of "who" words, one five-form verb. You've got this.
📝 QUICK RECAP · THE WHOLE WEEK ON ONE CARD

1. Conjugation = changing a verb's shape to match who does it (just like English am / are / is).

2. Subject pronouns = the "who" words: yo, tú, usted, él, ella, nosotros/nosotras, ustedes, ellos/ellas. Use usted to be polite.

3. SER (to be) — five forms:

WhoSERMeaning
yosoyI am
túeresyou are (familiar)
usted / él / ellaesyou are (polite) / he is / she is
nosotros / nosotrassomoswe are
ustedes / ellos / ellassonyou all are / they are
🎮 PLAY · MATCH THE PERSON TO THE SER FORM

Match each "who" with its SER form. Correct pairs lock in; clear the whole board.

🧠 SELF-CHECK · READY FOR THE QUICK CHECK?

Ungraded practice. Get all five and you're ready for the Module 13 quick check below.

🎤 OPTIONAL · ¿QUIÉN ERES TÚ? (SHORT FREE-WRITE / RECORDING)

Your teacher may ask for a short "who am I" clip or a few written lines. This is graded on effort and completeness, not perfection — and you may read from notes. Use only what you know this week:

• Me llamo… (My name is…)
• Yo soy… (a role: estudiante…)
• Soy de… (where you're from)
• Mi mamá / mi papá es… (a family member + their role)

Three or four SER sentences is plenty. Personality words like "funny" or "kind" come later in Module 17 — today just stick to names, roles, and origin.
