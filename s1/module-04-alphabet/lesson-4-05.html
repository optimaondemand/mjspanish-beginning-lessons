A recap, a letter memory game, a tongue-twister to record, a readiness check, and your Module 4 Quick Check.
📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · Spanish is phonetic

Each letter makes one steady sound, so you can read a new word out loud correctly. English spelling is the tricky one, not Spanish.

2 · 27 letters + the rolling rr

The alphabet has 27 letters, including ñ. The h is silent; a single r taps, and rr (or a starting r) rolls.

3 · Spanish in your world

US place names are Spanish you can now read: Los Ángeles (the angels), Santa Fe (holy faith), San Diego.

🎮 REVIEW GAME · LETTER MEMORY MATCH

Flip two cards to match each tricky letter with its Spanish name.

🎤 RECORDING TASK · TIER 2 (COMPLETION & EFFORT)

El trabalenguas — the R tongue-twister

Time to try that rolling rr for real. Here is a classic Spanish trabalenguas (tongue-twister). Read it slowly first, then a little faster:

Erre con erre, guitarra;
erre con erre, barril.
Rápido corren los carros
cargados de azúcar del ferrocarril.

Your task: use the Canvas media recorder to record yourself reading it aloud (about 20–40 seconds). Then submit.

  • Graded on effort and completion, not on a perfect roll — the trill is hard, and trying counts.
  • Read from the screen — no memorizing.
  • Record as many times as you like and submit your best take.
✅ SELF-CHECK · ARE YOU READY?

These mirror the Quick Check. If you can do these, you're ready. Practice as many times as you like.
