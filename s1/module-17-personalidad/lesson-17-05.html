📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · ser + adjective

To say what a person is like, use the verb you already own: Yo soy inteligente. Ella es amable.

2 · The endings agree

Ends in -o → flip to -a for a girl (generoso → generosa). Ends in -e → it stays the same (inteligente).

3 · Cognates are free

Most trait words are English twins: responsable, valiente, generoso. Watch the false friend simpático (= nice, not "sympathetic").
