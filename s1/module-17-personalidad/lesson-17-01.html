Lesson 17.01 · Welcome to Module 17
What is someone like? Say it in Spanish.
You already know the verb. This week you add the words that describe a person — and most of them you can already read.
👋 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
To say what a person is like, you already have the tool: the verb ser. This week you just add the describing words.
Back in Module 13 you learned ser — to be. You've used it to say who someone is. Now you'll use that same verb to say what someone is like: kind, funny, brave, generous. And here's the good news for a Monday: a huge number of Spanish personality words look almost exactly like their English cousins.
🔄 QUICK REWIND · YOU ALREADY OWN THIS VERB

Say each of these out loud — they came from Module 13. This is the verb ser (to be):

yo soy — I am tú eres — you are usted / él / ella es — you (formal) / he / she is nosotros somos — we are ustedes / ellos son — they / you all are

All week the pattern is simple: ser + a describing word. Yo soy… (I am…). Ella es… (She is…). You bring the verb; this module brings the adjectives.

★ WHY THIS MATTERS

Describing someone's character is more than vocabulary practice. When you can name what is good in a person — that they are generoso, valiente, honesto — you are learning to see and to honor virtue in others. That habit, in any language, is worth having.
