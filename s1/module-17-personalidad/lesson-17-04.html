🌎 Spanish in Your World

Twin words, split at birth

Long ago, Latin words for character traits flowed into both Spanish and English. That's why inteligente looks so much like intelligent, and responsable like responsible. They're not lucky coincidences — they're cousins, or cognates, sharing a grandparent in Latin.

Read these pairs. The Spanish word is on the left; you already know its English twin:

inteligente → intelligent
responsable → responsible
generoso → generous
tímido → timid
valiente → valiant
cómico → comic (funny)

One honest warning: a few look-alikes are false friends. Simpático looks like "sympathetic," but it really means nice / friendly. Most cognates are gifts; a couple are little traps — and today's game shows you both.
