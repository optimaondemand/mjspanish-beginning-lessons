Lesson 5.03
Ten words to make the new sounds real
New words on a rule you already own: the -o / -a gender pattern from Module 2.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Let's turn yesterday's three rules into muscle memory with ten short, useful words.
Every word below shows off one of your new consonant sounds. Listen, repeat, and flip each card to see its meaning and the sound it practices. Then you'll sort them el / la — the gender rule from Module 2, riding along on brand-new words.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
say ten Spanish words with clean B/V, D/T, and S/Z/C sounds.
2
sort each new word into el or la using its ending.
🔊 PART 1 · ESCUCHA Y REPITE (LISTEN & REPEAT)

Press ▶ to hear each word, then say it out loud. Tap a card to flip it and see the meaning + which sound it practices. Colors: blue = masculine (el), red = feminine (la).
