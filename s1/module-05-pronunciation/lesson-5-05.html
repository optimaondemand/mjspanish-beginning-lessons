📚 THE WHOLE MODULE IN THREE CARDS

1 · B and V are twins

Both make one "b" sound with the lips — never the English "teeth-on-lip" v. So vaca and boca begin alike.

2 · Soft D and T; one "S"

D and T are gentle, on the teeth, with no puff of air. And s, z, and c (before e/i) all say a plain "s" — the Latin American way.

3 · Your map is Spanish

Colorado = reddish, Nevada = snowy, Amarillo = yellow, Florida = flowery. Place names are descriptions in disguise.
