💡 Spot the gender pattern: it's buenos días (because el día is masculine) but buenas tardes / buenas noches (because la tarde and la noche are feminine). The -o/-a rule from Module 2 is quietly at work — the greeting's ending matches the noun's gender.

💪 ONE IDEA TO CARRY OUT

Greetings change with the clock (buenos días → buenas tardes → buenas noches) and goodbyes come in several friendly flavors. And the gender pattern you already know decided buenos vs. buenas for free.
