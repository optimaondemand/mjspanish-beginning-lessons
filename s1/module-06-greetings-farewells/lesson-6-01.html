🔍 PART 2 · SOMETHING YOU MAY NOT HAVE NOTICED

In English, you say "hi" to almost anyone — your best friend, your principal, a stranger. And there's only one word for "you": you. You use the same "you" for everybody.

Spanish is a little more careful. Read these two questions — they both mean "How are you?":

¿Cómo estás? (to a friend)
¿Cómo está usted? (to show respect)

Same meaning — but the second one is more respectful. That's because Spanish has two words for "you": a friendly one and a respectful one. Choosing the right one is a small act of good manners, and it's easier than it sounds.

💡 The big question of the week: when do you use the friendly "you," and when do you use the respectful one? Hold onto that question — tomorrow it gets a clear answer (and it's simpler than you'd guess).
