## Student task (as stated to the student)

Record yourself speaking **30–90 seconds in Spanish**, introducing **one real famous person from a Spanish-speaking background** (your choice — see the Lesson 18.04 gallery, or pick your own and check your facts). Present *to the camera*. You may **read from your notes**. Include, using the five frames from Lesson 18.05:

1. **Se llama __________.** (name)
2. **Es de __________.** (origin — *ser*)
3. **Es __________.** (job / identity — *ser*)
4. **Es __________ y __________.** (two personality traits — *ser* + adjectives, with correct -o/-a agreement)
5. **Le gusta __________.** (one thing they like — *gustar*)

**Scaffold (M365, Band B / grades 6–8):** draft the five sentences in **Word** first, save as `Persona-Famosa_YourName.docx` in the Spanish OneDrive folder, read it aloud a few times, then record.

## 4-Point Rubric (SpeedGrader)

Grade holistically on the **4-point scale** below. This is a gentle, effort-and-completeness rubric — **not** graded on perfect pronunciation.

| Score | Descriptor |
|---|---|
| **4 — Exceeds** | All five parts present and clear; correct verbs (*ser* for identity/traits/origin, *gustar* for likes); personality adjectives agree (-o/-a or -e handled correctly); speaks the full ~30–90 s clearly and audibly; shows real effort and pride. |
| **3 — Meets** | Four or five parts present; verbs mostly correct; minor agreement or wording slips that don't block meaning; length and clarity within range. **This is the target for most students.** |
| **2 — Approaching** | Two or three parts present, OR the recording is very short / hard to hear; several verb or agreement errors; meaning partly unclear. Invite a re-record. |
| **1 — Beginning** | One part present or off-task (e.g., in English, or not a presentation); or no usable audio. Reach out and offer support + a retake. |

**Feedback norm:** always name one thing done well and one specific fix, in warm grades-6–8 language. Offer a re-record for any score below 3 — the goal is a confident finish to Semester 1, not a gatekeeper.
