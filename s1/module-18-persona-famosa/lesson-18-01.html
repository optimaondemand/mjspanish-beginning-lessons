Lesson 18.01 · The last module of Semester 1
Look how far you've come
This week doesn't add new grammar. It brings the whole semester together into one thing you'll be proud of.
🎉 TODAY'S BIG IDEA
You can now say who someone is, how they are, what they like, who's in their family, and what they're like as a person. That's a whole person, in Spanish.
Module 18 is a review-and-celebrate week. There is nothing new to memorize. Instead, you'll dust off everything from the semester and use it to build your capstone: Una persona famosa — a short spoken presentation about a famous person you admire.
🧰 THE TOOLS YOU ALREADY OWN

Look at everything you picked up this semester. Every one of these goes into your capstone:

ser

who someone is: Es de México. Es actor.

estar

how / where they are: Está en Los Ángeles.

gustar

what they like: Le gusta la música.

la familia

relationships: Tiene una hija.

la personalidad

what they're like: Es generoso y valiente.

los números

age / facts: Tiene 40 años.

That is a remarkable amount for one semester. This week we simply put the pieces together.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · NO WRONG ANSWER
Start dreaming about your capstone. Who might you want to present? Pick the kind of famous person that interests you most.
🏆 Your Capstone

Una persona famosa

By Friday you'll record a short 30–90 second spoken presentation (about a minute) introducing a famous person in Spanish — who they are, where they're from, what they're like, and what they like. You'll write your notes first and read from them while you record; no memorizing, no pressure.

This week walks you there step by step: review the verbs (Day 2), review the describing words (Day 3), gather ideas and real examples (Day 4), then draft and record (Day 5).
