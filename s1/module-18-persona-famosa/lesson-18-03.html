📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday's verbs are the frame; today's words are the picture inside it — who's in the family, and what a person is like.
Refresh the family words from Module 11 and the personality words from Module 17. Then a memory game locks them back in for your capstone.

💬 PUT IT TOGETHER

Watch how the verbs and the words snap together into a real description — this is exactly what your capstone will sound like:

Se llama Frida. (Her name is Frida.)
Es de México. (ser — origin)
Es creativa y valiente. (ser — personality)
Le gusta el arte. (gustar — what she likes)
