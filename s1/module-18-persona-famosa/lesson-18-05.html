Lesson 18.05 · The finish line
Write it, then say it: Una persona famosa
Fill in the frames, practice out loud, and record. You'll read from your notes — no memorizing.
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Your whole capstone fits into five simple sentence frames. Fill in the blanks about your person, and you have a script.
Every frame uses Spanish you already own. Copy them, swap in your person's details, and you're ready to record.
✏️ YOUR FIVE SENTENCE FRAMES
Frame (fill in the blank)What it does · example
Se llama __________.the name — Se llama Frida Kahlo.
Es de __________.origin (ser) — Es de México.
Es __________.job / identity (ser) — Es pintora. / Es futbolista.
Es __________ y __________.personality (ser + 2 adjectives) — Es creativa y valiente.
Le gusta __________.what they like (gustar) — Le gusta el arte.
💡 Remember the endings: if your person is a girl, personality adjectives ending in -o flip to -a (generoso → generosa). Adjectives ending in -e (inteligente, valiente, amable) stay the same.

📝 Step 1 · Draft your notes in Word

Before you record, write your five sentences so you can read from them. Use Microsoft Word (in Canvas, open your OneDrive):

  1. Open Word and start a new document.
  2. Type your five sentences using the frames above.
  3. Save it in your Spanish folder as Persona-Famosa_YourName.docx.
  4. Read it out loud two or three times — fix anything that feels hard to say.

Optional: add one photo of your person to a PowerPoint slide if you'd like something on screen while you talk.

🎥 Step 2 · Record your capstone (the graded assignment)

In Canvas, open the assignment "Una persona famosa" — Semester 1 Capstone and use the built-in media recorder to record yourself:

  • Speak for 30–90 seconds (about a minute) in Spanish.
  • Introduce your famous person using your five sentences.
  • Read from your notes — that's allowed and encouraged.
  • You are presenting to the camera (not chatting with anyone) — record as many takes as you need and submit your best.

Graded on a friendly 4-point rubric for completeness and effort — not for perfect pronunciation. If you use the frames and speak clearly, you'll do great.

✅ QUICK SELF-CHECK BEFORE YOU RECORD

Make sure your sentences are solid. Practice as many times as you like.

📝 And: the short Semester 1 Check

Alongside your capstone, take the quick Module 18 / Semester 1 Check in Canvas — a friendly review of the whole semester (ser, estar, gustar, family, personality).

  • All review — nothing you haven't already practiced.
  • Unlimited tries, and your best score counts — it can even replace a weaker earlier score.
  • Score 70% or higher to close out Semester 1.
✓

Before you go: click "Mark as Done", submit your Persona famosa recording, and take the Semester 1 Check. ¡Lo lograste! (You did it!)

🎉 ONE SEMESTER, DONE

You started with one word, ¡Hola!, and you're finishing by describing a whole person out loud in Spanish. That's real progress — be proud of it. Semester 2 picks up right where you are. ¡Nos vemos!
