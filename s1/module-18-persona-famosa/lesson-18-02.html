📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Three verbs do most of the describing you'll ever need: ser, estar, and gustar. Each one has its own job.
Nothing new today — just a clean side-by-side so your capstone verbs come out right the first time.
📚 THREE VERBS, THREE JOBS
VerbIts jobExample for your capstone
ser
soy, eres, es, somos, son		who someone is — identity, origin, job, and personalityEs de México. Es director. Es creativo y valiente.
estar
estoy, estás, está, estamos, están		how someone feels right now, and where they areEstá en Los Ángeles. Hoy está contenta.
gustar
le gusta / le gustan		what someone likes (a "backwards" verb: use le gusta)Le gusta la música. Le gustan los deportes.
💡 Quick memory trick: ser = the permanent facts (from Mexico, a director, generous). estar = the right-now facts (in LA, happy today). gustar = likes.

💪 ONE IDEA TO CARRY OUT

Three verbs, three jobs. Ser for who they are, estar for how they are right now, gustar for what they like. Keep those three straight and your capstone is halfway written.
