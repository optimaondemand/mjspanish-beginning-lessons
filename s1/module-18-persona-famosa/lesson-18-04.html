🌿 A year-end custom: las doce uvas

In Spain and much of Latin America, New Year's Eve is called Nochevieja (literally "the old night"). At midnight, as the clock strikes twelve, many families eat twelve grapes — las doce uvas de la suerte (the twelve lucky grapes) — one for each chime, making a wish with each one. It's a real, widely-loved tradition — a warm way to close a year, just like we're closing this semester.
